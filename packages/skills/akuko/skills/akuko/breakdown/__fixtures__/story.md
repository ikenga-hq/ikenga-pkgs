# The Last Ferry

Mara reaches the river pier at dusk with a suitcase in one hand and a letter in the other. The ferryman, Oren, is already coiling the rope for the last crossing of the day.

"We're closed," he says. She holds up the letter. He reads it, folds it away, and says nothing.

Halfway across the river the engine coughs and dies, and fog closes in around the little boat. Oren kneels at the engine while Mara holds the lantern steady over his hands.

"My sister lived over there," she says. Oren nods, not looking up.

At the far pier a woman waits under a street lamp, her face hidden by the fog. Mara steps off the ferry. Oren turns the boat back into the fog.
