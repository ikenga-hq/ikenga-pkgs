akuko default board `@0.5`: people can see what they approve. Bible pictures, key frames, cards and image intake documents show as thumbnails that open larger in an accessible dialog (claude.ai `/_blob/` assets; on a local board, thumbnails `views/publish.ts` embeds, shrunk with ffmpeg when available, plus relative links; never a remote URL). Every started pipeline stage opens a lens in the middle pane and stays pressed; intake documents sit with the script, not the bible, and "your move" names the right place; "Review next shot" starts at the first waiting shot in film order; status and undo use the current stage's decisions only; undoing an approval akuko already folded shows the unit waiting again with its Approve; and once the current stage has nothing waiting, "your move" offers a copyable sentence to tell akuko you are done. Decisions from `@0.1`–`@0.4` boards still fold.
