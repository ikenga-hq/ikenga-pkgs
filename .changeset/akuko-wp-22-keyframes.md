akuko: stage 4, key frames. After the bible is approved, `/akuko keyframes` makes one final frame per shot, one shot at a time, from the approved character turnarounds, location plate and lookbook, with the sketch composition described in text. Every image goes through the spend gate (paid providers wait for a person to approve the amount; the manual provider is free); frames are reviewed on the board and only a person approves them. A frame made from a reference that was later sent back is flagged, never changed.
