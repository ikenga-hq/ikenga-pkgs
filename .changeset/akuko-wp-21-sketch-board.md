akuko sketch board (stage 2): free, deterministic framing-diagram panels drawn from each shot's blocking (thirds grid, labelled boxes, camera-move arrow, no figures or faces) and 9-panel beat boards per scene. Panels go through the review gate like any shot, and re-runs are idempotent. Adds `board/` and fills `/akuko board`.
