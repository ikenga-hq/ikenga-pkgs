A shot's treatment now decides its motion. Only shots a person marks `video` (or shots with no tier and no treatment, as before) get a paid clip; tier A and B shots default to a free camera move on the still and tier C holds. New `treat` and `moves` commands record a treatment and turn a shot's motion note into a gentle push-in, pull-back or drift. Key frame and clip jobs now send the shot's negative prompt, and both planners skip shots removed from the shot list.
