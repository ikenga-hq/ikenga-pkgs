akuko: pilot one part of a film end to end. `motion/cli.ts` and `lipsync/cli.ts` take `--part P7` on `plan`, `status` and `step`: a part opens for clips and lip-sync once its own key frames and the bible entries they use are approved, worked out from the approvals each time (never stored), and a refusal names what is missing. A scoped run plans, cuts, prices and makes only that part's shots, through the rules guard and the spend gate as before; the motion stage itself stays locked until the whole film opens, and `motion/cli.ts parts` lists every part. In the Resolve handoff a lip-sync now sits at its line's film time, with the shot's key frame (and its camera move, split across the pieces) held before and after it. Clips and lip-syncs record where they sit in film time as their job starts. Motion and lip-sync share one stage roll-up, which never counts idle clips (a shot that is no longer a video shot) and lets a motion stage with nothing to make reach in-review. The whole-film estimate imports the treatment table and prices lip-sync by the lip-sync step's own span rule. The default board is 0.4: idle clips are shown as idle and left out of "your move".
