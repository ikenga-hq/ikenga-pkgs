akuko: `assemble rough`, a free rough cut with sound. Each live shot in film order is exactly its length: the approved clip (a longer clip is cut at the shot's end, a shorter one holds its last frame), else the approved key frame with its gentle move, else black with the reason. Approved voice-over takes sit at each shot's start plus 0.1 s at -16 LUFS. Optional music (ducked under the voice), room tone and per-shot effects come from flags. The mix is limited and its loudness measured. A project with locked master audio is cut on the master's clock with the master laid untouched. `--dry-run` prints the per-shot plan. Like `assemble preview`, it is a by-product: never a review unit, nothing written to `akuko.json`, no stage moved. Picture lock is still a stub.
